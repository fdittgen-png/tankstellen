1. Overview

Sparkilo is a free, open-source fuel and EV charging price comparison app with optional trip recording. It is built local-first: everything the app knows about you lives on your phone unless you switch on a feature that says otherwise. There are no ads, no tracking pixels, no advertising identifiers and no analytics SDK. This policy is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR); it applies to every user, wherever you are.

The app is open source — every statement below can be verified in the code. A machine-readable summary of what is processed is kept in docs/privacy/data_inventory.json and the app's own tests fail if this page, the store declarations and the code drift apart.

Depending on how you use TankSync, the controller of your synced data changes:

  • No TankSync (default): nothing is stored on any server we operate. Your data stays on your device and you are in sole control of it.
  • Private database: you connect your own Supabase project. You are the controller; we never see that data.
  • Join a group: you connect a Supabase project operated by someone you know (family, friends). That person is the controller of the shared database.
  • Sparkilo Community: you connect the shared database operated by the developer named above (Supabase, hosted in the EU — AWS eu-central-1, Frankfurt). The developer is the controller; Supabase, Inc. is a processor bound by its data-processing terms.

The app tells you which of these applies before you connect, and shows it again in Settings → Privacy Dashboard.

  • Photos, videos or files beyond the receipt photo you choose to scan

Brand logos are bundled with the app. Loading them from the internet (logo.clearbit.com, which would receive your IP address) is off unless you switch it on in Settings → Privacy.

6. Data security

  • All network communication uses HTTPS (TLS in transit); the app refuses plain-HTTP sync endpoints.
  • API keys, your GitHub token and the TankSync session live in platform-native encrypted storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • Local databases are AES-encrypted with a key kept in the Keystore / Keychain — including recorded trips, vehicle data, service reminders and cached prices.
  • On the server, every table is protected by row-level security so an account can only ever read or delete its own rows; shared ratings and community price reports are the only rows visible to other signed-in users.
  • Error traces are scrubbed of e-mail addresses, coordinates and tokens before they are stored or sent.
  • No data is sent to any server unless you run a search, open a map, or enable TankSync / diagnostics.

7. Your rights

Under Articles 15–22 GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Each right has a button in the app:

  • Access — Settings → Privacy Dashboard shows every category stored on the device and, when connected, every row in your TankSync database.
  • Export (portability) — Privacy Dashboard → Export all my data writes one ZIP with a machine-readable JSON per category (favorites, alerts, profiles, itineraries, price history, vehicles, fill-ups, trips with GPS samples and one GPX per trip, baselines, service reminders, charging logs, your consent record) plus a JSON export of every server table.
  • Rectification — edit any entry directly in the app; the change syncs if TankSync is on.
  • Erasure — see Section 8.
  • Restriction / objection — switch off TankSync, trip sync, the tile proxy or diagnostics in Settings; revoke the location, Bluetooth, camera or notification permission in your device settings.
  • Withdraw consent — Settings → Privacy & data lists every consent with the date you gave it and the policy version you saw; each can be switched off, and the app stops the corresponding processing immediately.
  • Complain — you may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work or place of the alleged infringement. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first: fdittgen@gmail.com.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can delete everything yourself, directly in the app — no support request needed:

  • Local data: Settings → Privacy Dashboard → Delete all data removes every database the app keeps on the device — profiles, favorites, alerts, price history, cached prices, vehicles, fill-ups, recorded trips and their GPS samples, OBD2 baselines and adapter caches, service reminders, achievements, error traces, API keys, tokens and the home-screen widget data — and returns the app to its first-launch state.
  • TankSync account and server data: TankSync → Data Transparency → Delete account wipes every row you own in one transaction (favorites, alerts, ignored stations, price reports, content reports, vehicles, fill-ups, itineraries, baselines, ratings, trips, trip shares you gave or received, wait-time pings, sync settings, deletion records and your user row) and then deletes the account identity itself, including any linked e-mail address. If any table could not be erased the app tells you which, instead of claiming success. This cannot be undone.
  • Individual items: every favorite, alert, vehicle, fill-up, trip, price report and content report can be deleted on its own; deletions propagate to your other devices.

If you can no longer access the app, you can request deletion by contacting the developer (Section 11); please write from the e-mail address linked to your account so we can verify ownership. An anonymous account that was never linked to an e-mail cannot be identified by anyone — including us — without the device it was created on.

9. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you believe a child has created a TankSync account, contact us and we will delete it.
