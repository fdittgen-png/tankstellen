A 10-cent difference per litre doesn't feel like much. Multiply it by a full tank, then by every fill-up this year — that is what choosing the right station is actually worth.

Sparkilo shows you which station near you is cheapest right now, from each country's official price data, then helps you burn less of what you buy.

Free. No ads, no account, no tracking, open source.

WHY IT IS WORTH TRUSTING
• Official open-data prices, country by country — not crowdsourced guesses that send you on a wasted detour.
• Petrol, diesel, LPG, CNG, E85 and EV charging on one map, in one search.
• Cheapest first: pins are coloured cheap-to-expensive and the winner carries a Cheapest badge.
• Your GPS position and any API keys never leave your device. No ads, no account, no tracking — and the source code is public.

SAVE ON THE PRICE
• Search nearby or along your entire route. Filter by fuel type, radius, open now, amenities and highway-only.
• Route mode finds the best stop inside your travel corridor, so the detour pays for itself.
• Price alerts, per station or by radius, computed on your device and firing only when a deal is genuinely near you.
• Favourites show every fuel price at a glance; EV chargers show kW, connectors and live availability.

THEN SAVE ON THE LITRES
• Consumption tracker: tank level and range, average L/100 km, cost per km, total spent, and a fill-up history with deltas.
• Log fill-ups by hand, or scan the pump display or the receipt with OCR.
• Trip logbook records hands-free over an OBD2 ELM327 adapter, or with GPS alone, and colours your route by efficiency.
• Eco-coaching turns throttle and RPM histograms into habits you can actually change, compared month over month.
• Carbon dashboard: cost and CO2 broken down by trip length and speed band.

WHILE YOU DRIVE
• Live-price overlay for the station you are approaching.
• Home-screen widget, Android Auto, and spoken price announcements.
• Basic, Medium and Full presets keep the app as simple or as deep as you want. Multiple driver profiles supported.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Privacy Dashboard: see, export or delete everything, at any time.
• Optional TankSync syncs favourites, vehicles, fill-ups and alerts to a server you control — not to ours.

COUNTRIES WITH OFFICIAL LIVE PRICES
Austria, Argentina, Australia, Chile, Denmark, France, Germany, Greece, Italy, Luxembourg, Mexico, Portugal, Romania, Slovenia, South Korea, Spain and the United Kingdom. EV charging worldwide via Open Charge Map.
