# Screenshots

Live screenshots of **Sparkilo**, captured on a real device in the **French** locale against the live `Prix Carburants` (France) source, app version **6.0.5+2026705942**. The UI is fully localised — these are the French strings; every other language renders the same screens.

The constant bottom bar is **Favoris · Carte · [Recherche] · Carburant · Trajets**, with the round green **Search** button raised in the centre notch. Settings lives behind the gear icon in the top-right, not in the bar.

> Every image below is also used in the [User Guide](User-en-Home), placed beside the paragraph it illustrates. Raw assets live in [`guide/`](https://github.com/fdittgen-png/tankstellen/wiki) (720 px, content-cropped) and in [`screenshots/`](https://github.com/fdittgen-png/tankstellen/wiki) (older captures at 1080×2316).

## Finding fuel

### Search results
Live prices sorted by Distance / Price / A–Z / 24 h, with the official open-data source named at the top and a freshness warning chip when the newest price is over an hour old.

<img src="guide/search-results.jpg" width="360" alt="Search results — nearby stations with prices, trend arrows and freshness">

### Search criteria — nearby
Fuel chips, radius slider, open-now, amenity and brand filters, plus *Save as defaults*.

<img src="guide/search-criteria-nearby.jpg" width="360" alt="Criteria sheet for a nearby search">

### Search criteria — along a route
Start, waypoints, destination, segment length, max detour and minimum saving.

<img src="guide/search-criteria-route-1.jpg" width="360" alt="Criteria sheet in route mode">

### Station detail
The full per-fuel price table, opening hours, region, amenities, payment methods, your private rating and the local 30-day price history.
