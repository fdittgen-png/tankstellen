# The Sparkilo name and logo

**"Sparkilo" and the Sparkilo logo are trademarks of Florian DITTGEN.**
The [AGPL](LICENSE) gives you the code. It does not give you the name —
no free software licence does, and §7(e) of the AGPL says so
explicitly.

This applies to the user-facing name. The repository is called
`tankstellen` and the Android application id is
`de.tankstellen.fuelprices` for historical reasons; the mark is
**Sparkilo**, which is what a user sees.

## What you may do without asking

- Say your product is **built on Sparkilo**, **compatible with
  Sparkilo**, or **a fork of Sparkilo**, as a statement of fact.
- Use the name in documentation, articles, talks, comparisons and
  academic work.
- Keep the name and the logo when you build Sparkilo **unmodified** for
  your own use, or for a distribution such as F-Droid that builds from
  this repository.
- Use the name in the URL of a page *about* Sparkilo.

## What needs permission

- Shipping a **modified** Sparkilo under the name Sparkilo, or under a
  name or logo close enough to be confused with it.
- Using the name or logo as the name of your company, product, service
  or app-store listing.
- Registering a domain whose main element is "sparkilo", or a social
  account that reads as official.
- Implying endorsement, partnership or certification.

## Why

A fork is welcome — that is what the licence is for. But a person who
installs something called Sparkilo should get the thing this project
stands behind. If a modified build carries the name and misbehaves, the
complaint arrives here, and neither we nor the person can tell the
difference.

**Rename your fork and everything else in the licence still applies to
you**, including the right to sell what you build.

To ask for permission, write to the address in the repository's GitHub
profile.
